# Spell Identity reads ticks from a table and learns the schools of hits

Spell Identity tells which spell made Your Hit from the player's casts, the Hit's time, school and Line Kind, and for a tick the Tick Rhythm on its Nameplate; there is no link from a cast to a Nameplate. A tick takes its spell from a built-in table of the spells that tick (`src/TickSpells.lua`: each rank's spell ID, its interval and school, and whether it travels first), made from the Classic Era spell data for every rank that Forever's trainers teach. The first plan learned the tick facts too, from Hits that only one cast spell could explain, but in the Warlock Traces nearly every tick had several such spells (Shoot, Shadow Bolt, curses and other DoTs come within a few seconds of each tick), so about 25 of about 313 spell Hits got an icon; learning from every spell that could explain a tick gave wrong icons, as a steady rotation lines a wrong spell up with the ticks again and again. With the table, about 270 got one and none was wrong. A ticking spell that is not in the table gets no icon, and a new class or patch needs a new table row. A direct hit or a shot takes the one cast of the last 1.5 s that fits it, and needs the spell's school: from the table for a spell that also ticks, or else learned as a Spell Fact, which counts once two Hits agree on it; a Hit that only one cast explains and that disagrees starts it again, as a new wand changes Shoot's school. Facts are per spell ID, so a new rank learns again, and the player can clear them (`/nsctr forget`, or a button in the Icons options). The pet's casts count against a Hit's spell but never for it, so a pet's Hit teaches nothing. The facts are saved in `db.global`.

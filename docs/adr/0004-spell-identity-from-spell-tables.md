# Spell Identity reads spells from tables, and learns only a wand's school

Spell Identity tells which spell made Your Hit from the player's casts, the Hit's time, school and Line Kind, and for a tick the Tick Rhythm on its Nameplate; there is no link from a cast to a Nameplate. It reads the spells from two built-in tables made from the Classic Era spell data for every rank that Forever's trainers teach: the spells that tick (`src/TickSpells.lua`: each rank's interval, school and longest duration, whether its cast also hits, and whether it travels first) and the spells that hit directly (`src/DirectSpells.lua`: each rank's school). A tick fits a spell one interval after the spell's cast, or after its landing on that Nameplate for one that travels and hits, then on the spell's Tick Rhythm until its duration ends; two ticks of one school at once on one Nameplate, such as Fireball's and Ignite's, get none. A direct hit or a shot takes the one cast of the last 1.5 s that fits it, of a spell in a table whose school fits. Only casts of spells that can hit compete: the direct table, which also holds the pets' damage spells, and the ticking spells whose cast also hits, or for a miss any ticking spell, as its application can miss. A pet's cast counts against a Hit's spell but never for it; a buff's cast, a Voidwalker's Torment, or a DoT that only ticks does not count at all, so it cannot make a Shadow Bolt unsure, and a proc with no cast of its own, such as a poison's, gets no icon instead of the buff's.

The first plan learned these facts instead, from Hits that only one cast spell could explain. In the Warlock Traces nearly every tick had several such spells (Shoot, Shadow Bolt, curses and other DoTs come within a few seconds of each tick), so about 25 of about 313 spell Hits got an icon, and learning from every spell that could explain a tick gave wrong icons, as a steady rotation lines a wrong spell up with the ticks again and again. Learning direct hits let a buff cast learn the school of the procs that came after it, and then show its icon on them. With the tables, about 290 got one and none was wrong. A spell that is not in a table gets no icon, and a new class, patch or Forever spell needs a new table row.

Only a wand's Shoot learns its school, as a Spell Fact, because the wand sets it: a fact counts once two Hits agree on it, and a Hit that only one cast explains and that disagrees starts it again, as a new wand changes it. The player can clear it (`/nsctr forget`, or a button in the Icons options). The facts are saved in `db.global`. A Hit carries no source, so a pet's Hit with no cast seen can still be taken for the player's spell when that spell's cast is the only one in its window and its school fits.

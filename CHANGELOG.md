# Changelog

## 1

- Type `/nsctr record` to record your combat events, then type it again to stop. Attach the recording to a bug report, so problems in groups, raids, and battlegrounds can be fixed.
- Your damage and your pet's damage now show above enemy nameplates, and so do misses and dodges. Your combat log filter needs to show them. Spell icons come in a later release.
- The game's own damage numbers over the unit you hit are hidden while the addon is on. Turn off "Hide Blizzard Target Text" to keep them. Your own setting comes back when you log out or turn the addon off.
- Numbers finish their animation when an enemy's nameplate disappears, so you see your killing blow.
- If your enemy nameplates are off, a chat message tells you once to press V.
